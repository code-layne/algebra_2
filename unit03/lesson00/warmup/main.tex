\small These skills power today's lesson on reading polynomial graphs. Work quickly.

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{1. Powers of a negative number.} Watch the signs.
\[
  (-2)^2 = \blank{1.2cm} \qquad (-2)^3 = \blank{1.2cm} \qquad (-2)^4 = \blank{1.2cm}
  \qquad -2^4 = \blank{1.2cm}
\]
Complete the rule: an \textbf{even} power of a negative number is \blank{2.4cm}; an \textbf{odd}
power \blank{3.0cm} the sign.

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{2. Evaluate a cubic.} Let $f(x) = x^3 - 3x + 2$. Complete the table.
\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
$x$ & $-2$ & $-1$ & $0$ & $1$ & $2$ \\ \hline
$f(x)$ & \blank{0.9cm} & \blank{0.9cm} & \blank{0.9cm} & \blank{0.9cm} & \blank{0.9cm} \\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Read your outputs left to right: they go \emph{up}, then \emph{down}, then \emph{up} again. A
straight line never does this and a parabola turns only once --- so this graph must turn
\blank{1.4cm} times.

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{3. Zeros from a factored form.} The \textbf{zeros} of a function are the inputs that make it
$0$. Set each factor equal to $0$ and solve.
\[
  g(x) = (x-1)^2(x+2): \qquad x = \blank{1.4cm} \quad \text{and} \quad x = \blank{1.4cm}
\]
