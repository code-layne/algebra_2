Look at your outputs for $x=-1$ and $x=3$ (compute $f(3)$ in your head). They are equal! The inputs
$-1$ and $3$ sit the same distance from $x=\blank{0.8cm}$, the middle. This mirror effect is
\textbf{symmetry}.
