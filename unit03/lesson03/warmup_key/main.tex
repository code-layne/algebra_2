\begin{notesbox}{Getting Ready: Skills We'll Use Today}
Three things you already know. Today we \emph{divide} polynomials, and these are the moves
inside every step --- work quickly, then check with a neighbour.

\vspace{6pt}
\textbf{1. Long division with numbers.} Divide $247 \div 5$.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=6pt]
  \item Quotient: \ans{$49$} \qquad Remainder: \ans{$2$}
  \item Fill in the check: \; $247 = 5 \cdot$ \ans{$49$} $+$ \ans{$2$}
  \item And as one number: \; $\dfrac{247}{5} = 49 + $ \ans{$\dfrac{2}{5}$}
\end{itemize}

\vspace{8pt}
\textbf{2. Divide the leading terms, then subtract.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=8pt]
  \item $\dfrac{6x^3}{2x} = $ \ans{$3x^2$} \qquad $\dfrac{-8x^2}{x} = $ \ans{$-8x$}
        \qquad $\dfrac{x^4}{x} = $ \ans{$x^3$}
  \item Subtract --- the minus reaches \emph{every} term:
        \; $(x^3 + 5x^2) - (x^3 - 2x^2) = $ \ans{$7x^2$}
  \item $(4x^2 - 3x) - (4x^2 + 8x) = $ \ans{$-11x$}
\end{itemize}

\vspace{8pt}
\textbf{3. Multiply, then read it backwards.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=8pt]
  \item Multiply: \; $(x - 2)(x^2 + 3x + 1) = $ \ans{$x^3 + x^2 - 5x - 2$}
  \item So when $x^3 + x^2 - 5x - 2$ is divided by $x - 2$, the quotient is \ans{$x^2 + 3x + 1$}
        and the remainder is \ans{$0$}.
  \item Today's question: if nobody hands you the multiplication, how do you \emph{find} that
        quotient? Write your guess: \ans{divide --- long division}
\end{itemize}
\end{notesbox}
