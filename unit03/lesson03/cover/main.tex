% Keep in Mind is a CONTENT summary. It matches the Closing Practice's Watch-out line.
\boxguard[8]
\begin{remindbox}
\small
Dividing polynomials follows the statement $P(x) = D(x)\cdot Q(x) + R$: the
\textbf{dividend} equals the \textbf{divisor} times the \textbf{quotient}, plus the
\textbf{remainder}, so every answer $Q(x) + \dfrac{R}{D(x)}$ can be checked by multiplying back.
\textbf{Long division} works for any divisor; \textbf{synthetic division} works only for
$x - r$, and uses $r$ --- for $x - 2$ that is $2$, for $x + 2$ it is $-2$. A remainder of $0$
means the divisor divides the polynomial evenly. \textbf{Watch out:} before you divide, write a
$0$ for every missing power --- $x^3 - 8$ is $1,\ 0,\ 0,\ -8$ --- or every coefficient after
the gap lands in the wrong column.
\end{remindbox}
