\begin{notesbox}{Getting Ready: Skills We'll Use Today}
Three things from Lesson 3.4. Today we read a polynomial's graph \emph{between} its arms ---
where it meets the axis, where it turns, and where it rises and falls. Work quickly, then check
with a neighbour.

\vspace{8pt}
\textbf{1. Degree, leading coefficient, end behavior.} For $f(x)=-2x^5+3x^2-1$:

\vspace{4pt}
\hspace*{1.5em}degree $=$ \ans{5} \qquad leading coefficient $=$ \ans{$-2$}

\vspace{6pt}
\hspace*{1.5em}as $x\to-\infty$, $f(x)\to$ \ans{$+\infty$} \qquad as $x\to+\infty$, $f(x)\to$ \ans{$-\infty$}

\vspace{10pt}
\textbf{2. Symmetry.} Is $g(x)=x^4-3x^2+7$ even, odd, or neither? \ans{even}

\vspace{4pt}
\hspace*{1.5em}How do you know? \ans{only even powers ($7=7x^0$), so $g(-x)=g(x)$}

\vspace{10pt}
\textbf{3. Zeros from factors.} Solve $(x-1)^2(x+3)=0$.

\vspace{4pt}
\hspace*{1.5em}$x=$ \ans{1} \quad and \quad $x=$ \ans{$-3$}

\vspace{6pt}
\hspace*{1.5em}How many times does the factor $(x-1)$ appear? \ans{twice}

\vspace{6pt}
\hspace*{1.5em}The graph of $y=(x-1)^2(x+3)$ meets the $x$-axis at both zeros. Do you think it
\emph{crosses} the axis at both? \ans{not at $x=1$}
\end{notesbox}
