\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{Part C --- Explain a step} \hfill \emph{4 points}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em, start=8]
  \item Maya simplified $(3x^2 + 2x - 5) - (x^2 - 4x + 1)$ like this:
    \[
      3x^2 + 2x - 5 - x^2 - 4x + 1 \;=\; 2x^2 - 2x - 4
    \]
    Her answer is wrong. Name the step where she went wrong, explain what she should have done
    there, and give the correct answer.

    \vspace{4pt}
    \gradedblank[id=gs8, type=rubric, points=4,
      display={She went wrong removing the parentheses: subtracting a polynomial subtracts
        \emph{every} term, so each sign in $x^2 - 4x + 1$ flips, not just the first. The step
        should read $3x^2 + 2x - 5 - x^2 + 4x - 1$, so the answer is $2x^2 + 6x - 6$.}]{\linewidth}{1.3in}
\end{enumerate}
