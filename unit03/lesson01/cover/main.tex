% Keep in Mind is a CONTENT summary — it matches the Closing Practice's Watch-out line on the deck.
\boxguard[8]
\begin{remindbox}
\small A polynomial in \textbf{standard form} runs from the highest power down, and its
\textbf{degree} is that highest power. To subtract a polynomial, the minus sign changes the sign
of \emph{every} term inside the parentheses, not just the first. To multiply, every term meets
every term; with three or more factors, multiply two at a time. A power of a sum is repeated
multiplication: $(x+3)^3=(x+3)(x+3)(x+3)=x^3+9x^2+27x+27$, never $x^3+27$.
\end{remindbox}
