\begin{notesbox}{Getting Ready: Skills We'll Use Today}
Three quick wins. Today we read a polynomial's \emph{shape} from its formula --- work quickly,
then check with a neighbour.

\vspace{6pt}
\textbf{1. Synthetic division (Lesson 3.3).} Divide $x^3-2x^2-5x+6$ by $x-1$.
\begin{work}
  \begin{array}{r|rrrr}
    1 & 1 & -2 & -5 & 6 \\
      &   & 1  & -1 & -6 \\ \hline
      & 1 & -1 & -6 & 0
  \end{array}
\end{work}
Quotient: \ans{$x^2-x-6$} \qquad Remainder: \ans{$0$}

\vspace{10pt}
\textbf{2. Powers of a negative.} Simplify each.

\vspace{4pt}
$(-x)^2=$ \ans{$x^2$} \qquad $(-x)^3=$ \ans{$-x^3$} \qquad $(-x)^4=$ \ans{$x^4$}
\qquad $(-x)^5=$ \ans{$-x^5$}

\vspace{6pt}
If $f(x)=x^2+3$, then $f(-x)=$ \ans{$x^2+3$}. \; Is $f(-x)$ the same as $f(x)$? \ans{yes}

\vspace{10pt}
\textbf{3. Standard form.} Write $g(x)=5x-3x^4+2-x^2$ with its powers from highest to lowest.

\vspace{4pt}
$g(x)=$ \ans{$-3x^4-x^2+5x+2$}

\vspace{6pt}
The highest power of $x$ is \ans{$4$}, and the number in front of that term is
\ans{$-3$}.

\vspace{6pt}
\emph{Hold on to this:} the first term \emph{written} in $g$ was $5x$. Which term do you think
controls the shape of the graph far to the left and right? \ans{$-3x^4$}
\end{notesbox}
