\textbf{1. Which roots actually exist?} Evaluate each one. If there is no real answer, write
``not a real number.''
\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.5}
\begin{tabular}{@{}l l@{}}
(a) \; $\sqrt{36}=$ \blank{3.2cm} & (b) \; $\sqrt{-36}=$ \blank{3.2cm} \\
(c) \; $\sqrt[3]{8}=$ \blank{3.2cm} & (d) \; $\sqrt[3]{-8}=$ \blank{3.2cm} \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Exactly one of the four has no real answer. Which one --- and what is it about \emph{squaring} that
makes it impossible? \writeline

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{2. Solve each inequality.} (Watch the last one: dividing or multiplying by a negative
\emph{flips} the sign.)
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), leftmargin=2.1em, itemsep=3pt]
  \item $x-3\ge 0$ \quad$\Rightarrow$\quad \blank{3.0cm}
  \item $2x+8\ge 0$ \quad$\Rightarrow$\quad \blank{3.0cm}
  \item $5-x\ge 0$ \quad$\Rightarrow$\quad \blank{3.0cm}
\end{enumerate}
