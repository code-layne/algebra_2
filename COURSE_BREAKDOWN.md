## Organizing idea

Each unit is one **function family** —
linear → quadratic → polynomial → rational → radical → exponential → logarithmic. Every
unit opens with **Lesson X.0, "Characteristics of ____ Functions,"** which re-teaches the
full read-a-graph toolkit built so far and then adds the characteristics this family is
the first to require. That cumulative spine (§3 of the plan) is the course's backbone:
asymptotes debut in Unit 4, origin symmetry in Unit 3, restricted domain in Unit 5, and
so on.

**Out of scope:** conic sections, sequences & series, probability & statistics,
trigonometry, linear systems / linear programming.

## What ships per lesson and per unit

**Per lesson (13 `main.tex` work products):** lesson plan, cover, warm-up, notes,
activity, exit ticket, homework — each of the last five with an answer key — plus a
slide deck.

**Per unit:** binder cover, unit cover, `tests/{practice_test,actual_test}` and their two
keys, with the practice pair published to `sample_test/` + `sample_test_key/`.

Test shape (Unit 1's pattern, followed since): Part A Vocabulary (matching, 8 pts),
B Multiple Choice (6 items incl. SOL-style, 12 pts), C Short Answer & Computation
(8 items, 40 pts), D Extended Response (2 justify items, 12 pts). Practice and actual are
parallel with different numbers and contexts.
