\begin{remindbox}
\small \textbf{Factoring} is un-multiplying: it reverses the FOIL that built a trinomial. To \emph{solve}
$ax^2+bx+c=0$, get a $0$ on one side, factor the other side completely, and use the \textbf{Zero Product
Property} --- if a product is $0$, one of its factors must be $0$. That turns one quadratic equation into
two easy linear ones. The values you find are the \textbf{roots} (a.k.a.\ zeros or solutions): the exact
spots where the parabola crosses the $x$-axis. Golden rule: move everything to one side \emph{first} ---
the Zero Product Property is a fact about \emph{zero}, not about any other number.
\end{remindbox}
