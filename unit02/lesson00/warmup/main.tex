\textbf{3. Spot the pattern.} Complete the table for $y = 2x - 4$, then answer below.
\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
$x$ & $0$ & $1$ & $2$ & $3$ \\ \hline
$y$ & \blank{0.9cm} & \blank{0.9cm} & \blank{0.9cm} & \blank{0.9cm} \\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Each time $x$ increases by $1$, $y$ changes by the \emph{same} amount. That constant change is
\blank{2.2cm}. \; We will call it the \textbf{slope}.
