\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{1. Read the points.} Write the coordinates $(x,y)$ of each labeled point on the grid.
\begin{center}
\begin{minipage}{0.42\linewidth}\centering

  $A = ($\ans{2}$,$\;\ans{3}$)$ \\[10pt]

  $C = ($\ans{0}$,$\;\ans{$-2$}$)$
\end{minipage}
\end{center}

\vspace{2pt}
\textbf{2. Evaluate a function.} Let $f(x) = 2x - 4$. Find each output, then write it as a point
$(x, f(x))$.
\[
  f(-1) = \ans{$-6$}\quad(\text{point: }\ans{$(-1,-6)$})
  \qquad
  f(0) = \ans{$-4$}\quad(\text{point: }\ans{$(0,-4)$})
  \qquad
  f(3) = \ans{$2$}\quad(\text{point: }\ans{$(3,2)$})
\]

\vspace{2pt}
\textbf{3. Spot the pattern.} Complete the table for $y = 2x - 4$, then answer below.
\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
$x$ & $0$ & $1$ & $2$ & $3$ \\ \hline
$y$ & \ans{$-4$} & \ans{$-2$} & \ans{$0$} & \ans{$2$} \\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Each time $x$ increases by $1$, $y$ changes by the \emph{same} amount. That constant change is
\ans{$+2$}. \; We will call it the \textbf{slope}.
\end{notesbox}

\begin{teachernote}
Item~3 is the bridge to the whole lesson: the constant $+2$ in the table \emph{is} the slope and the
reason the graph is a straight line. If students write the points from item~2 and notice they lie on
that same line, even better --- that is the picture the Guided Notes open with.
\end{teachernote}
