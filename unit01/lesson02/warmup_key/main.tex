\begin{notesbox}{Getting Ready: Skills We'll Use Today}
Three things you already know. Today's notes and practice lean on all three --- work quickly,
then check with a neighbour.

\vspace{6pt}
\textbf{1. Distance from zero.} Evaluate each.
\[
  |{-7}| = {\color{keyred}\mathbf{7}} \qquad |4| = {\color{keyred}\mathbf{4}} \qquad
  |{-3}| + |5| = {\color{keyred}\mathbf{8}} \qquad -|{-6}| = {\color{keyred}\mathbf{-6}}
\]
How many different numbers have an absolute value of $7$? \ans{two} \; Name them:
\ans{$7$ and $-7$}

\vspace{8pt}
\textbf{2. Distance between two numbers.} Use the number line.
\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[x=0.62cm, y=0.56cm]
  \numline{-6}{12}
  \fill[forest] (3,0) circle (3.0pt);
  \fill[forest] (11,0) circle (3.0pt);
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}
\vspace{-4pt}
How far apart are $3$ and $11$? \ans{$8$} \; Write that distance with absolute-value bars:
\ans{$|11-3|$} \\[6pt]
How far apart are $-2$ and $6$? \ans{$8$} \; With bars: \ans{$|6-(-2)|$} \; And how far is
$x$ from $6$? \ans{$|x-6|$}

\vspace{8pt}
\textbf{3. Shade the number line.} Use a \emph{filled} dot when the endpoint is included and an
\emph{open} dot when it is not.

\vspace{2pt}
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\linewidth}
\centering (a) all numbers with $x \ge -1$ \\[4pt]
\begin{tikzpicture}[x=0.56cm, y=0.56cm]
  \numline{-5}{5}
  \draw[very thick, keyred] (-1,0)--(5.5,0);
  \fill[keyred] (-1,0) circle (3.0pt);
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\linewidth}
\centering (b) all numbers between $-3$ and $2$ \\[4pt]
\begin{tikzpicture}[x=0.56cm, y=0.56cm]
  \numline{-5}{5}
  \draw[very thick, keyred] (-3,0)--(2,0);
  \draw[keyred, thick, fill=white] (-3,0) circle (3.0pt);
  \draw[keyred, thick, fill=white] (2,0) circle (3.0pt);
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{minipage}

\vspace{6pt}
Which of your two pictures is \emph{one} connected piece? \ans{both are} \; Could a shaded picture
ever come in \emph{two} separate pieces? \ans{yes}
\end{notesbox}
