\boxguard[8]
\begin{remindbox}
\small
$|x-h|$ is the \textbf{distance} between $x$ and $h$, so it is never negative, and two different
numbers sit the same distance out --- one on each side. That is why $|X|=c$ has \emph{two}
answers. \textbf{Isolate the bars before you split.} \emph{Less th\textbf{AND}} means
\emph{closer than}: one interval. \emph{Great\textbf{OR}} means \emph{farther than}: two rays.
Pick the wrong one and you do not get a slightly different answer --- you get the
\emph{opposite} set.
\end{remindbox}
