the label.
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{1pt}
  \item Lightest box that still passes? \blank{2.0cm} \qquad Heaviest? \blank{2.0cm}

An absolute value is a \emph{distance}. Every problem today asks how far an expression is from a

\boxguard
\begin{notesbox}{1. Absolute Value Is a Distance}
$|x|$ is the distance of $x$ from $0$, so it is \textbf{never negative}. The question $|x|=5$ asks
\emph{which numbers are exactly $5$ units from zero} --- and there are \blank{1.0cm} of them:
\[ x = \blank{1.2cm} \quad\text{or}\quad x = \blank{1.2cm}. \]
\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.5]
  \numline{-6}{6}
  \fill[forest] (-5,0) circle (3.5pt); \fill[forest] (5,0) circle (3.5pt);
  \draw[<->, forest!70, thick] (0,0.6) to[bend left=18] node[above, font=\scriptsize]{$5$} (5,0.6);
  \draw[<->, forest!70, thick] (-5,0.6) to[bend left=18] node[above, font=\scriptsize]{$5$} (0,0.6);
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}
\textbf{Big idea:} a single distance corresponds to \emph{two} inputs --- one on each side of zero.
That is the whole engine of this lesson.
\end{notesbox}

\begin{notesbox}{2. Solving $|ax+b| = c$ --- The Two-Case Rule}
\textbf{Step 1: isolate} the absolute value. \textbf{Step 2:} if $c \ge 0$, split into two cases:
\[ ax+b = c \qquad\text{or}\qquad ax+b = -c. \]
\textbf{Try it:} $|2x - 3| = 7$.
\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.4}
\begin{tabularx}{0.92\linewidth}{@{}X | X@{}}
\textbf{Case 1 (positive):} & \textbf{Case 2 (negative):} \\
$2x - 3 = 7$ & $2x - 3 = -7$ \\
$x = \blank{1.6cm}$ & $x = \blank{1.6cm}$ \\
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
\textbf{Verify} both in the original: $|2(\blank{0.7cm})-3| = 7$ \checkmark \; and
$|2(\blank{0.7cm})-3| = 7$ \checkmark. Graphically, these are the two $x$-values where $y=|2x-3|$
meets the line $y=7$.
\end{notesbox}

\begin{notesbox}{3. Isolate First --- and Two Special Cases}
You \emph{cannot} split until the bars stand alone. Solve $3|x+1| - 2 = 10$:
\begin{work}
  3|x+1| - 2 &= 10 \\
      3|x+1| &= 12 \\
       |x+1| &= 4 \\
         x+1 &= 4 \quad\text{or}\quad x+1 = -4 \\
           x &= 3 \quad\text{or}\quad x = -5
\end{work}
\textbf{Special cases} (explain each from the distance meaning):
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt]
  \item $|x - 4| = -3$ has \blank{3.2cm}, because a distance can never be \blank{2.0cm}.
  \item $|x - 4| = 0$ has exactly \blank{2.0cm} solution: $x = \blank{1.0cm}$ (distance $0$ means the expression \emph{is} zero).
\end{itemize}
\end{notesbox}

\boxguard
\begin{notesbox}{4. Inequalities --- The ``And'' Case ($<$, $\le$)}
$|x| < 3$ asks \emph{which numbers are within $3$ of zero} --- everything \textbf{between} $-3$ and
$3$. \textbf{``Less th\emph{AND}''}: $|X| < c \iff -c < X < c$ (one connected interval).

\textbf{Solve} $|2x - 1| \le 5$:
\begin{work}
        |2x - 1| &\le 5 \\
  -5 \le 2x - 1 &\le 5 \\
      -4 \le 2x &\le 6 \\
       -2 \le x &\le 3
\end{work}
Write the solution three ways:
\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
\begin{tabularx}{0.95\linewidth}{@{}l l X@{}}
\textbf{Set notation:} & $\{\,x \mid \blank{3.0cm}\,\}$ &
\textbf{Interval:} $\blank{2.4cm}$ \\
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.5]
  \numline{-4}{5}
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}
{\footnotesize Shade \emph{between} the two bounds; use \textbf{closed} dots because of ``$\le$''.}
\end{notesbox}

\begin{notesbox}{5. Inequalities --- The ``Or'' Case ($>$, $\ge$)}
$|x| > 3$ asks \emph{which numbers are more than $3$ from zero} --- everything \textbf{outside}
$[-3,3]$. \textbf{``Great\emph{OR}''}: $|X| > c \iff X < -c$ \textbf{or} $X > c$ (two rays).

\textbf{Solve} $|x + 2| \ge 4$:
\begin{work}
  |x + 2| &\ge 4 \\
    x + 2 &\le -4 \quad\text{or}\quad x + 2 \ge 4 \\
        x &\le -6 \quad\text{or}\quad x \ge 2
\end{work}
Write the solution three ways:
\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
\begin{tabularx}{0.95\linewidth}{@{}l l X@{}}
\textbf{Set notation:} & $\{\,x \mid \blank{3.4cm}\,\}$ &
\textbf{Interval:} $\blank{3.2cm}$ \\
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.5]
  \numline{-8}{4}
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}
{\footnotesize Shade the two \emph{outside} rays; the interval uses $\cup$ (``union'').}
\end{notesbox}

\begin{practicebox}
Solve $|3x - 6| > 9$ and write the solution set in all three forms.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=3pt]
  \item Split (``or''): $3x - 6 < \blank{1.0cm}$ or $3x - 6 > \blank{1.0cm}$.
  \item Solve: $x < \blank{1.2cm}$ or $x > \blank{1.2cm}$.
  \item Set: $\{\,x \mid \blank{3.2cm}\,\}$; \quad Interval: $\blank{3.4cm}$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{practicebox}
