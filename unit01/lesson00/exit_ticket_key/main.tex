  \item \textbf{Classify.} List \emph{every} set each number belongs to (natural, whole, integer,
        rational, irrational, real).

        \vspace{3pt}
        $\sqrt{36}$ \; is \ans{$6$: natural, whole, integer, rational, real}

        \vspace{6pt}
        $\sqrt{6}$ \; is \ans{irrational, real (only)}

  \item \textbf{Name the property} that allows each step.
        \begin{center}
        \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.7}
        \begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}l X@{}}
        \hline
        \textbf{Step} & \textbf{Property} \\
        \hline
        (a) \; $7(x+3)=7x+21$ & \ans{distributive property} \\
        (b) \; $2x-9=5 \;\Rightarrow\; 2x-9+9=5+9$ & \ans{addition property of equality} \\
        (c) \; $4+(-4)=0$ & \ans{additive inverse property} \\
        (d) \; $6x=24 \;\Rightarrow\; \dfrac{6x}{6}=\dfrac{24}{6}$ & \ans{division property of equality} \\
        \hline
        \end{tabularx}
        \end{center}
        Two of those four changed only \emph{one} side and two changed \emph{both}. In one sentence,
        say how a property of real numbers differs from a property of equality.
        \ansline{A property of real numbers rewrites a single expression into an equal one (here (a)
        and (c)); a property of equality acts on \emph{both} sides at once to make a new equation
        with the same solutions (here (b) and (d)).}

  \item \textbf{SOL-style multiple choice.} What is the solution to $-5x \ge 20$?
        \begin{work}
          -5x &\ge 20 \\
            x &\le -4
        \end{work}
        \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*., leftmargin=1.9em, itemsep=1pt]
          \item $x \ge -4$
          \item $x \le -4$ \quad \textcolor{keyred}{\textbf{$\leftarrow$ correct}}
          \item $x \ge 4$
          \item $x \le 4$
        \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
