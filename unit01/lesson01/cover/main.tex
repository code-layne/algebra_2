\begin{learningtargetbox}
\small
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=5pt]
  \item \ldots{} work out how fast something is changing from any two facts about it --- a graph,
        a table, or two receipts.
  \item \ldots{} build a rule for a steady situation even when nobody tells me the starting
        amount.
  \item \ldots{} recognise when two rules that \emph{look} different are actually the same rule,
        and rewrite one into the other.
  \item \ldots{} say what changes about a straight-line graph when I change a number in its rule.
\end{enumerate}
\end{learningtargetbox}
