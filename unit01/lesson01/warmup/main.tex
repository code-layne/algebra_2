\textbf{1. Count the steps.} Two points on the line below are marked.
\begin{center}
\begin{minipage}{0.40\linewidth}\centering
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.56]
  \lgrid{-2}{6}{-3}{7}
  \draw[very thick, forest] (-1.5,-3)--(5.5,7);
  \fill[charcoal] (1,-1) circle (4pt) node[below right, font=\tiny, charcoal]{$(1,-1)$};
  \fill[charcoal] (4,3) circle (4pt)  node[above left,  font=\tiny, charcoal]{$(4,3)$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{minipage}%
\begin{minipage}{0.56\linewidth}\raggedright
  Going from $(1,-1)$ to $(4,3)$, the graph moves \blank{1.4cm} to the right
  and \blank{1.4cm} up. \\[6pt]
  So for every \emph{one} step right it moves \blank{1.6cm} up. \\[6pt]
  Where does this line cross the vertical axis? \blank{1.8cm}
\end{minipage}
\end{center}

\vspace{12pt}
\textbf{2. Solve for $y$.} Rearrange each equation so that $y$ stands alone on the left.
\[
  2x + 3y = 12 \;\Longrightarrow\; y = \blank{3.0cm}
  \qquad\qquad
  5x - y = 8 \;\Longrightarrow\; y = \blank{2.6cm}
\]

\vspace{12pt}
\textbf{3. Distribute, then tidy up.} Multiply out and then get $y$ by itself.
\[
  y - 5 = 3(x - 2) \;\Longrightarrow\; y = \blank{2.8cm}
  \qquad
  y + 1 = -2(x + 4) \;\Longrightarrow\; y = \blank{3.0cm}
\]

\vspace{12pt}
{In items 2 and 3 you started with two equations that \emph{looked} nothing alike and ended
with something much easier to read. Hold on to that.}
