\begin{headlinebox}{forestbg}
\small\textbf{One In, One Out --- Three Families, One Set of Questions.} Every item on this page
asks one of the same six questions: \emph{is it a function? what is the domain? the range? the
zeros? the intercepts? how fast is it changing?} With your partner, say out loud which question you
are being asked \emph{before} you start computing. Argue about the ones you disagree on first.
\end{headlinebox}

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{Part 4 --- Two tests, two questions.} The vertical line test decides whether a graph is a
function. Now consider a \textbf{horizontal} line test.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), leftmargin=1.9em, itemsep=5pt]
  \item The graph of $y=x^2$ is crossed \emph{twice} by the horizontal line $y=4$. Is $y=x^2$ still
        a function? \blank{1.6cm} \; Why does that not break the promise? \writeline
  \item The graph of $x=3$ is crossed \emph{once} by every horizontal line, and it still is not a
        function. What exactly does each test measure? \writelines{2}
\end{enumerate}
