% ── Unit overview ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=forestbg,colframe=forest,boxrule=0.9pt,arc=2mm,
    left=4mm,right=4mm,top=3mm,bottom=3mm]
  \textbf{Unit Overview.}\quad
  Unit 1 reactivates and deepens the Algebra 1 skills that underpin every topic in
  Algebra 2. Students review core calculation strategies using Desmos, reinforce their
  understanding of equations and inequalities, and reconnect with the language of
  functions and data. This unit sets the foundation for the transformational study of
  function families that follows in Units 2--10.
\end{tcolorbox}

% ── Lessons in this unit ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────

\begin{skillbox}[Lessons in This Unit]{goldbox}

  \textbf{\#} & \textbf{Title} & \textbf{Focus} \\
  \hline
  1 & Expressions \& Operations Review
    & Order of operations, evaluating expressions, using Desmos to simplify,
      factor, and apply exponent rules (Algebra 1 SOL: A.EO.1--4) \\
  \hline
  2 & Equations \& Inequalities Review
    & Linear equations and inequalities, literal equations, systems of equations,
      solving quadratics; discriminant (Algebra 1 SOL: A.EI.1--3) \\
  \hline
  3 & Functions \& Statistics Review
    & Linear, quadratic, and exponential functions; key features; writing equations;
      bivariate data and regression (Algebra 1 SOL: A.F.1--2, A.ST.1) \\
  \hline
\end{tabularx}
\end{skillbox}

  A.EO.3 & Derive and apply the laws of exponents. \\
  A.EO.4 & Simplify and determine equivalent radical expressions. \\
  \hline
  A.EI.1 & Represent, solve, and interpret multistep linear equations and inequalities
           in one variable; literal equations. \\
  A.EI.2 & Represent, solve, and interpret systems of two linear equations/inequalities
           in two variables. \\
  A.EI.3 & Represent, solve, and interpret quadratic equations in one variable. \\
  \hline
  A.F.1  & Investigate, analyze, and compare linear functions algebraically and
           graphically; model linear relationships. \\
  A.F.2  & Investigate, analyze, and compare characteristics of quadratic and
           exponential functions; model these relationships. \\
  \hline
  A.ST.1 & Apply the data cycle with a focus on bivariate scatterplots and
           determining the curve of best fit using linear and quadratic functions. \\
